The machine-readable register is [acquisition-evidence-2026-09-08.json](../../research/literature/acquisition-evidence-2026-09-08.json). Only bibliographic details, bounded paraphrases and the hash are retained in the repo, not the source document or personal contact details. The report's described 5–500 kHz range and roughly 140 kHz peak are historical device attributes from a failed trial, not receive-band or treatment requirements. The report suggests lower-frequency work, but that suggestion does not establish safety or efficacy. Its apparent initial protection could reflect several confounded causes; do not label the entire result proven habituation.

Software duty/limiter limits are normalized-sample test budgets only. Physical output stays inhibited. |
| SG5 | Sustained efficacy: preregistered replicated sham/no-output comparison, independent feeding and stock-loss outcomes, repeat-exposure follow-up, uncertainty and harm assessment | Farm/ecology/statistics reviewers. Startle, fewer sightings or generic observed feeding is not prevented loss. |
| SG6 | Flora track: specified actual species/endpoint, benefit and harm evidence, replicated controlled design controlling light/nutrients/mixing/temperature | Separate ecology research decision. Present evidence does not justify a generic growth program; this gate does not block passive monitoring. |

Scientific and permission gates are not closed by passing synthetic tests. A pressure microphone measures neither all relevant particle motion nor animal safety; digital RMS/peak measures neither underwater SPL nor exposure dose.

## Access limits

Nature pages redirected to an identity endpoint; open full-text Europe PMC XML supplied the two primary studies and meta-analysis. Ordinary PMC/PubMed pages returned cookie/recaptcha pages. The shell-crushing publisher and Palermo repository returned 403; those claims remain abstract-level. No downloaded data was re-labeled as local field evidence. No beneficial marine flora claim was verified; that is a bounded review result, not proof no such effect could ever exist.
