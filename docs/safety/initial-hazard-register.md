# Initial hazard register

Status: P0/P1 desk baseline, all hazards open, 2026-09-07

This register identifies hazards; it does not prove a control works. Severity, likelihood, risk class, and acceptance criteria remain unassigned until the operating context and qualified owners are set. An agent or document cannot accept physical, ecological, electrical, marine, legal, or product risk.

## Authorization boundary

No purchase, hardware build, pressure test, electrical energization, field recording, site visit, installation, vessel/diving work, animal exposure, acoustic output, deployment, or shipment is authorized by this register. The current software prototype is offline and file-based. It must have no interface to a projector, amplifier, actuator, sensor, camera, radio, or other field hardware.

Any later output-capable hardware must default to physical inhibit and startup silence. Software alone cannot remove the inhibit. Acoustic activity requires a separate dated authorization, calibrated protocol, competent facility/site, independent stop, approved abort rules, and every applicable permission.

## Review and closure rules

1. Before a phase introduces exposure, its qualified owners assign severity, likelihood, risk criteria, control owner, due phase, and evidence standard.
2. A control described in prose remains unverified until its test evidence passes. A purchased CE-marked module does not close final-system hazards.
3. Changes to site, intended use, hardware, firmware, calibration, waveform, mount, battery, update path, or claims trigger hazard-impact review.
4. A critical unresolved hazard blocks the applicable design freeze, trial, deployment, or release. Schedule pressure and a working demo are not acceptance evidence.
5. Stop inhibits emissions and other hazardous outputs but preserves diagnostics and recovery where safe. Recovery never automatically rearms output.
6. Closure records link the exact configuration, test report, reviewer, date, residual risk decision, and operating limits.

The [production requirements register](../requirements/production-requirements.md) provides the linked verification matrix. All current physical and acoustic thresholds remain TBD with named decision owners.
