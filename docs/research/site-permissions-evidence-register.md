## Desk-preparation outputs before any request

1. Product owner confirms the intended farm and names the authorized site contact.
2. Farm partner and ecology reviewer complete the site facts above, including exact coordinates and species/loss evidence.
3. Data lead and safety owner bound the passive collection method in the [collection protocol](passive-audio-video-collection-protocol.md).
4. Current Croatian authority names, forms, submission route, review time, fees, required maps, method statements, data plan, and reporting conditions are checked at the time of application.
5. Each request separates farm consent, protected-area research permission, installation/navigation permission, data/privacy approval, and any later acoustic authorization. One approval must not be treated as covering the others.
6. Product owner approves every outgoing message and application. Draft research requests are stored in [`prior-trial-follow-up-drafts.md`](prior-trial-follow-up-drafts.md); they have not been sent.

No site activity may rely on this desk register alone. The controlling requirements are `REQ-SITE-001`, `REQ-SITE-002`, and `REQ-PERM-001` in the [production requirements register](../requirements/production-requirements.md).

### What constitutes a closed permission entry

An issued decision must have its actual reference number, issuing authority/competence, authenticated copy, issue/validity dates, applicant, site/zone, allowed method/equipment/protocol, conditions, reporting/advance-notification requirements, reviewer and change/expiry check. A submitted application, manager email or a historic trial's permit is insufficient. `Not applicable` requires a reviewed basis for the exact activity, not a blank field.

The [study-permission dossier](../compliance/study-permission-dossier.md) supplies the populated routing, attachment, decision and pre-work records. **PRE-01 currently fails because approvals are absent or applicability unresolved.** Every request still needs product-owner approval before sending.

Domestic permission is separate from [EU conformity](../compliance/regulatory-applicability-2026-09-08.md). CE/RED compliance does not authorize reserve research or acoustic exposure; a Croatian permit does not establish conformity. For PERM-008, no single generic acoustic permit or universal safe underwater SPL was verified. Authorities and qualified acoustic/ecology reviewers must determine the exact applicable acts and conditions. Acquisition owns scientific endpoints and exposure protocol; hardware/specialists own physical limits. No threshold is invented here.
