## Evidence already in hand

The official [ARIEL Croatian regional report](https://ariel.adrioninterreg.eu/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/ARIEL_D.T2.4.1_Pilot_Activities_-regional_Report_HRVate.docx) describes a 2019 Lim Bay study at two mussel-farm concessions using six DDD03L devices. Initial protection did not persist: severe predation was reported by mid-July, and the July repeat also failed after about two weeks. The authors concluded that the tested pingers, specified as 5–500 kHz with a peak around 140 kHz, were non-functional against fish predation.

That finding is negative evidence against repeating the tested device or treating its output as a design target. The report does not clearly describe randomized sham/no-output controls. Temperature, time, habituation, stock condition, site effects, and other causes therefore need separation. Its discussion of lower-frequency physiology does not establish a safe or effective treatment frequency or source level.

The [PREDADOR project page](https://www.pole-mer-bretagne-atlantique.com/ressources-biologiques-marines/predador) reports prior deterrence work and habituation but lacks enough protocol, control, calibration, and duration detail for a Poseidon specification.

## Draft REQ-ARIEL-001

**Subject:** Research data request: 2019 Lim Bay seabream pinger pilot

To the current ARIEL Croatian pilot data custodian,

We are preparing a passive-first research plan for monitoring gilthead seabream predation at shellfish farms. We reviewed the ARIEL Croatian regional report describing the 2019 Lim Bay DDD03L trials at two concessions. We understand that the tested devices did not prevent intense predation later in the May deployment or in the July deployment.

Before designing any new intervention study, we would like to understand that negative result and avoid repeating the same approach. If sharing is permitted, could you provide or identify:

1. the full protocol, dated deployment logs, site layout, device placement, depth, and device operating/maintenance checks;
2. longline assignment method and whether untreated, sham/no-output, or other concurrent controls existed;
3. raw or least-aggregated predation observations by concession, longline, date, mussel size, and treatment;
4. water temperature and other environmental or farm-operation records used in the interpretation;
5. device calibration or manufacturer output records, duty pattern, measured received levels, and evidence that each unit continued operating as intended;
6. fish observations, exposure duration, distance assumptions, habituation observations, and non-target effects;
7. analysis code, statistical methods, exclusions, uncertainty, and unpublished follow-up results;
8. data license, confidentiality limits, citation terms, and a suitable contact for technical questions.

We will treat any shared material under the stated access and citation terms. This request does not assume that another acoustic program is effective or safe.

Regards,

## Draft REQ-PREDADOR-001

**Subject:** Research methods and data request: PREDADOR acoustic-deterrence trials

To the current PREDADOR project data custodian,

We are reviewing prior acoustic-deterrence evidence before deciding whether an active trial should be designed. The public PREDADOR page reports trials in Brest/Quiberon, an estimated acoustic range, and observed habituation on mussel longlines.

If sharing is permitted, could you provide or identify:

1. the full protocol, sites, dates, species, farm configuration, assignment method, sample size, baseline, and follow-up duration;
2. untreated and sham/no-output controls, blinding, randomization, replication, and outcome definitions;
3. transducer, amplifier, waveform, duty pattern, calibration chain, source/received measures with reference units, geometry, and uncertainty;

5. event, feeding, stock-loss, or other outcome data by treatment and time;
6. habituation definition, time course, repeat-exposure results, and alternative explanations;
7. non-target observations, adverse outcomes, abort rules, and permission or environmental conditions that governed the work;
8. raw or least-aggregated data, analysis code, reports/publications, data license, and citation terms.

We will not treat behavioral response, short-term absence, or an estimated range as proof of sustained stock-loss reduction. We will follow the stated access and citation terms for any shared material.

Regards,
