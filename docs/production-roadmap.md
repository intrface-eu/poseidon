# Poseidon Trident: current-state audit and production completion plan

> Audit sections are a historical snapshot of repository evidence on 2026-09-07. The roadmap and gates remain the approved production plan; current status must be read from the repository and requirements register.
>
> **Evidence update, 2026-09-07:** After the audit snapshot, the official [ARIEL Croatian regional pilot report](https://ariel.adrioninterreg.eu/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/ARIEL_D.T2.4.1_Pilot_Activities_-regional_Report_HRVate.docx) was directly reviewed. It reports a 2019 Lim Bay trial at two mussel-farm concessions using six DDD03L devices. Initial protection did not persist: severe predation was reported by mid-July, and the July repeat also failed after about two weeks. The authors concluded that the tested pingers were non-functional against fish predation. Treat the tested 5–500 kHz range and roughly 140 kHz peak only as attributes of that failed historical device, not as Poseidon design targets. The report does not clearly document randomized sham controls; temperature, habituation, and other causes remain confounded, and raw data and fuller methods are still needed. This result is directly relevant negative evidence against reusing that device or claiming efficacy. It does not prove that every acoustic approach is ineffective and does not justify a lower frequency or source level without controlled safety and efficacy evidence. This update supersedes the snapshot statements below that the pilot site and outcomes were unknown; those statements remain only to preserve the dated audit record.

## Context

Poseidon is at the concept and research-planning stage, not the prototype stage. The user requested an evidence-based audit and a start-to-finish completion plan covering research, 3D models, electronics, software, validation, and production.

Confirmed scope: use only this repository as evidence; build the full platform in stages; target the EU/EEA first; assume a solo builder with no budget set. The first intended region is Limski kanal/zaljev on the Istrian coast, Croatia, with orada as the target predator. Confirm the scientific identification, expected to be gilthead seabream (*Sparus aurata*), with the farm/ecology partner. Sea-flora stimulation is a separate, research-gated track, not a condition for the first release.

This document is an audit and execution roadmap, not evidence of implementation, biological efficacy, permissions, or product conformity. Approving it does not authorize purchases, contacting third parties, deploying equipment, or acoustic exposure trials.

## 2. Recommended product boundary and architecture

| ID | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | First useful product | A passive monitoring unit that records likely feeding events, synchronized visual evidence, environmental conditions, and device health. Detection alone must not be presented as prevention. |
| D2 | Acoustic intervention | SIREN remains electrically inhibited until acoustic engineering, environmental review, permissions, and controlled efficacy trials pass. Do not hard-code dolphin imitation as the solution. A failed efficacy gate blocks deterrence claims, not continued monitoring research. |
| D3 | Compute and deployment | Begin with one Raspberry Pi CPU target and Linux ARM64. Add an accelerator only if measured workloads miss the accepted performance/power budget. Use proven enclosure components before designing a custom pressure vessel. |
| D4 | System arrangement | Above-water hub/radio/power compartment, wired underwater hydrophone/camera head, separately gated projector, and above-water solar REEF nodes with wired wet probes. Final mounting and power choice depend on the farm survey. Tethered lab power is not proof of field autonomy. |
| D5 | Software baseline | Python services, C/C++ ESP32 firmware, TypeScript/Next.js UI using Bun. Recommend Mosquitto, FastAPI, PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB, SQLite offline cache, ChirpStack, Docker Compose, and one selected EU-region object-storage service. | D6 | Safety and lifecycle | Local fail-silent control must not depend on the cloud. Use an independent hardware output inhibit and bounded local state machine. A stop inhibits emissions but preserves diagnostics/recovery where safe; rearming is explicit. Signed, recoverable updates are a production requirement for both hub and firmware, not a future hook. |

There is no code to reuse. Reuse the existing component names, monorepo layout, and site/zone/device topic structure after reconciling their contracts. Reuse research questions, not unverified component selections or claimed measurements.

### Findings from external source checks that affect the plan

ARIEL's December 2020 report lists the Croatian Institute of Oceanography and Fisheries and RERA SD among its partners (PDF p.3), and a Croatian seabream pinger pilot (p.19). It does not establish that the trial was in Lim Bay or report its efficacy. Requesting the underlying report/data is higher priority than inventing a new deterrent sequence.

The PREDADOR project page reports trials in Brest/Quiberon, an estimated acoustic range, and observed habituation on mussel longlines. It lacks enough protocol, control, and duration information to validate a Poseidon design. Treat it as prior-art evidence and a lead for follow-up, not a performance specification.

For the planned EU launch, the Commission gives CRA vulnerability/incident reporting applicability from **11 September 2026**, and main obligations from **11 December 2027** [S7]. RED cybersecurity requirements under 2022/30 currently apply; their repeal under 2026/339 takes effect **11 December 2027**, not upon its April 2026 publication [S8]. Maintain a dated applicability matrix and recheck at design freeze and launch.

### Critical path and conditional branches

The critical path is site/species/access definition, local passive evidence, safe and effective intervention evidence, design freeze, DVT/conformity, PVT, then release. Coding speed cannot remove ecological, permission, manufacturing, or seasonal gates.

R8 proceeds separately through literature review, controlled study, and an explicit include/stop decision. Localization, accelerators, custom PCBs, custom pressure vessels, and extra product variants enter only when a measured requirement justifies them. A negative gate results in a documented stop or revised scope, not an indefinitely pending task.
