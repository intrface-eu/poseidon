## Access and currency rules

- `READ`: official page or named legal provisions retrieved and read. This does not mean every linked document or every amendment was read.
- `INDEX`: an official page or official-domain search result identifies the instrument or provision; full legal text was not accessible. It is a lead for professional verification, not a claim that the full instrument was reviewed.
- `LIMITED`: the retrieved page lacks the detail needed for a decision, or retrieval failed. The limitation is named below.
- EUR-Lex HTML, ELI and PDF requests for RED, CRA, 2026/339 and batteries returned empty content. A direct Python `urllib.request` check returned HTTP **202**, `text/html`, **0 bytes** for the CRA PDF, RED text and Batteries ELI URLs. These are not downloaded legal texts. Searches against official EUR-Lex indexes and readable Commission pages support the bounded desk findings below; consolidated law, annexes, amendments and applicable standards still need a retained copy and adviser/lab review before design freeze.
- ECHA and Council pages identified below returned HTTP **403**. No authentication or access-control bypass was attempted. Official-domain search excerpts are explicitly `INDEX`, not a full-page review.
- Narodne novine pages were accessible. For long laws, direct in-memory Python HTML extraction was used to read the specified articles after WebFetch summaries truncated them. No legal-source files, authority responses or signed decisions were fabricated or uploaded.
- Access date is not legal commencement. A ministry guide is not a consolidated statute. Recheck at application, design freeze and market placement; preserve the retrieved version, hash, date and review scope in the released dossier. No claim is made that the search proves absence of every later amendment.
