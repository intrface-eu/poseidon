# Software support window — unapproved statement

No commercial release date, supported-release table, support end date, funded update service, responsible manufacturer or operational response promise is established by this repository. There is no claim that the current development revision is a supported product. No security-support window starts merely because this draft or a test result exists.

The existing [CRA assessment](../regulatory-applicability-2026-09-08.md), TIME-02/04/05, and [source register](../official-source-register.md), EU-05/06, distinguish three clocks:

1. **2026-09-11:** Article 14 reporting begins for relevant products, with its own transition. Do not wait for general conformity requirements to assess reporting duties.
2. **2027-12-11:** main CRA requirements apply, subject to product scope and transition/substantial-modification rules. This is not a project release date or a support expiry date.
3. Support normally lasts **at least five years**, or the expected-use period where legitimately shorter; longer expected use must inform the chosen period. Article 13(9) security updates remain available for **ten years after issue or the remainder of support, whichever is longer**. Update availability, support duration, service and warranty are different obligations.

These statements follow the existing desk assessment, whose full-text/adviser review remains open. They do not establish CRA conformity or legal approval. The [technical file](../technical-file-index.md), TF-15/LIFE-01, remains unapproved/unfunded; the [intake draft](vulnerability-intake.md) does not create a monitored reporting channel. No release, market supply, stakeholder contact or publication is authorized by this statement.
