## 4. PVT: process validation and second assembler (`G-PVT`)

**Entry:** DVT accepted for the exact candidate; manufacturing partner and independent assembler identified; frozen pilot count and selection basis, process/fixtures/station qualification, incoming/assembly/provision/calibration/EOL/packaging routes, rework limits, record control, approved cost and yield/rework acceptance rules. All numeric PVT quantities/limits are currently TBD and block approval.

1. Quality freezes the batch ID, authorized quantity, variants, lots/suppliers, shifts/stations/operators, record cutoff, inclusion/exclusion rules and first-pass/final acceptance definitions before build. Identify destructive qualification specimens and reference fixtures separately; no successful-piece cherry-picking. If a study uses multiple configurations, separate their denominators and justify pooling rather than hiding differences.
2. Supply the second assembler (not the designer) the exact controlled package: released BOM, vendor instructions, drawings with tolerances/material/finish/fastener/seal process, wiring/pin views, tools/fixtures, safety/hold points, provisioning/recovery method, calibration/EOL criteria, forms, NCR/rework and packaging/service instructions. Record package digest and assembler competence/training evidence; quality decides independence. No actual assembler is appointed here.
3. The designer may answer safety questions but must not invisibly complete work. The assembler logs each missing instruction, ambiguity, requested clarification, answer, time lost and any designer intervention. Stop at missing safe limits. An undocumented intervention fails the documentation-reproducibility demonstration for that step; after controlled revision, repeat the affected route independently and retain the first result.
4. Run actual incoming, assembly, provisioning, calibration, EOL and packaging on every pilot unit under the approved plan. Maintain unit/lot/station identity and immutable attempt logs. Record labor, parts consumed, actual cycle time and dwell/wait separately, NCRs/rework/scrap, station downtime and supplier issues. Physical proof must include real unit IDs and raw records; simulation never becomes a pilot unit.
5. Have the independent assembler perform the approved service/repair reproduction on the specified specimen using documents, with authorization and requalification requirements. Conduct actual forward/reverse lot/version/credential-reference traceability and recall-population reconciliation drills on controlled records; never use raw keys. Record missing links and closure evidence.
6. Reconcile unit outcomes to all started units and material/labor/cost to source records. Quality independently recomputes metrics below, reviews unresolved defects and the frozen economic criteria, and records pass/fail/blocked. No threshold may be relaxed retrospectively to turn the batch green; a changed criterion needs a new justified plan and evidence.
7. Close corrective actions and repeat affected process validation after changes. Freeze the manufacturing release package only after its acceptance; retain actual yield/cost and second-assembler proof. Commercial shipment still needs P9 permissions/conformity/support/user authorization, not an automatic EOL flag.

### Actual yield evidence structure (`YLD-01`)

The template contains **no units and null counts**, not a zero-unit successful batch. A real ledger holds one row per started unit and links all station attempts with identity, sequence, timestamps, result, raw evidence and NCRs. The batch summary must be derived from this ledger, not hand-entered percentages.

At the frozen reporting cutoff define:

- `N_started`: all distinct actual units entering the approved pilot route, including later failed, held or scrapped units. Track authorized-but-not-started quantity separately; it is not fabricated production.
- `N_accepted`: started units with all required completed accepted checks and final independent quality disposition under the frozen configuration.
- `N_scrapped`, `N_held`, `N_in_process`: mutually exclusive remaining unit dispositions. Reconcile `N_started = N_accepted + N_scrapped + N_held + N_in_process`; a missing unit is an error, not an exclusion.
- `N_first_pass_accepted`: subset of accepted units whose required stations all passed on first valid attempt without repair, rework or corrective retest. Preserve station abort/invalid-run rules established before the batch, and report all aborts/invalid attempts separately. Do not use an invalid-run label to hide a product failure.
- First-pass yield = `N_first_pass_accepted / N_started`; final accepted yield = `N_accepted / N_started`; rework incidence = distinct started units receiving rework divided by `N_started`; scrap proportion = `N_scrapped / N_started`. All are undefined if the denominator is absent or zero. At an interim cutoff label yields provisional and report holds/WIP; release requires their disposition, not silent exclusion.

Also report station first-pass outcomes with their own entrants/attempt denominators, defect counts by cause/lot/station/configuration, rework attempts/hours/parts, unplanned designer assistance, final accepted unit cost, batch size and exposure limits, uncertainty/statistical method and criterion source. Multiple defects per unit can exceed unit counts; do not add station percentages to obtain overall yield. Use a quality/statistics-reviewed confidence method when making production-yield claims; a small pilot's point estimate is not guaranteed future production performance.

Freeze sample size and acceptance/rejection rules with manufacturing/quality/statistics and the budget owner before the build. No pilot quantity, percent yield target, confidence bound, allowed rework count or cost ceiling is approved here. Actual pilot evidence is wholly missing.
