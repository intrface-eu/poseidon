## 1. Intake, isolation and retrieval (`SVC-01`)

**Owners:** service coordinator records the request; qualified electrical/mechanical/marine operations owners decide safe handling and retrieval; farm/site owner and authorities control access; security/privacy owners control data and credentials. No unqualified operator may improvise a repair or dive.

1. Open intake with actual serial/configuration, symptom, date/time/time quality, reported environment and operating state, customer contact in a restricted store, visible damage/leak/battery condition and relevant logs. Record reports as allegations until investigated. Link a safety or security incident immediately when suspected; do not wait for warranty eligibility.
2. Assess urgent hazards without asking the reporter to open, energize, charge, operate or retrieve the unit. Provide only approved safe instructions from the applicable product/site method. Future hazardous outputs follow an independently verified inhibit/isolation route; a remote UI acknowledgement is not physical isolation. Preserve diagnostics only where safe. Unknown battery, pressure or energy state requires specialist quarantine/handling, not an assumed discharge time or seal opening.
3. Before retrieval, require exact location/access and applicable permissions, competent operator roles, approved weather/sea-state/visibility limits, vessel/lifting/diving method where applicable, attachment/retrieval load path, exclusion area, communications, emergency/rescue plan and stop conditions. Limits and emergency contacts are currently unassigned and block the work. Hardware `PV-04` and HAZ-008/009 govern. Do not use a sensor cable or printed instrument mount as a lifting point.
4. Record authorization review and pre-work briefing. The approved method controls isolation, attachment release, guarded handling and contamination/battery precautions. Stop for changed weather, damaged gear, unknown loads, lost communications, trapped/entangled fauna or a deviation outside the approved method. Escalate to competent personnel rather than ad hoc recovery.
5. At handover, record actual unit/module serials, chain of custody, time, as-retrieved condition and photographs without exposing sensitive site/customer information. Contain leakage and batteries using specialist-approved handling and transport; do not rinse, vent, charge or dry using a generic recipe. Record packaging and transport approvals before any movement.
6. If equipment is lost or cannot safely be recovered, open an incident: last verified location/time, identity, tether/attachment status, battery/material hazards, navigation/ecological concerns, site notifications required by the approved plan and accountable recovery owner. Authority contact or field action needs separate authorization; inability to retrieve is not permission to send an unqualified person.

A retrieval drill in simulation records only a tabletop result. Actual timed recovery, loads, weather performance and operator competence need physical evidence and independent review.

## 2. Repair, recalibration and return to service (`REP-01`)

1. Quarantine and identify the returned unit; preserve configuration, media and as-found evidence before destructive diagnosis where safe. Handle private audio/video under an approved lawful basis, access restriction, retention and deletion plan. Do not copy media to a public issue or personal storage.
2. Qualified personnel approve safe inspection/diagnosis and a fault hypothesis. Reference parts do not supply physical repair limits. Seal reuse, allowable housing/window defects, torque, cleaning agents, corrosion removal, solder rework count, battery replacement and connector mating limits all require exact supplier/engineering instructions. **Unknown limits block the repair.**
3. Use an approved `NCR-01`/`ECO-01` repair instruction. Record replaced part/serial/lot, removed part disposition, actual labor, cumulative cycles, tools, firmware/configuration changes, credential actions and all attempts. Preserve unit history; replacing a wet head or board creates a parent/child link, not a fresh undocumented unit. Review suspect supplier lots across other units.
4. Engineering selects post-repair tests before work. Opening/changing a wet boundary requires the approved seal/closure and leak/pressure requalification decision; changing receive chain/clock/probe/gain requires calibration impact review; power/radio changes require affected electrical/EMC review; firmware/identity changes require authentication/update/recovery review. No improvised pressure test is allowed.
5. Execute approved tests only after separate authorization. Record as-left state and full evidence. Restore data only through the approved tested recovery process; revoke/rotate a removed or compromised module's credentials without putting secrets in the traveler. Quarantine or scrap unrepairable units through approved environmental/security handling.
6. Independent quality and domain owners decide internal return-to-service disposition. Give the user the changed configuration, supported versions, calibration validity, operating restrictions and revised maintenance due basis. Applicable site permissions, safe reinstallation method and user authorization remain separate gates; never automatically redeploy or rearm.

## 3. Preventive maintenance and spare parts (`SP-01`)

Maintenance interval, maximum unattended interval, wear limits and service life are **TBD** pending actual envelope/endurance/fouling/drift evidence (`PV-03`–`PV-10`, roadmap V6). The seven-day engineering gate and proposed 30-day field gate do not establish a commercial maintenance interval. Keep required due dates null until approved; lack of a due date blocks a supported service claim.

| Spare family | Reference need, not an order list | Required release evidence / owner |
|---|---|---|
| Matched seal/penetrator/closure kits | Exact purchased wet boundary only; no generic O-ring substitution | Vendor part/lot/shelf-life, material/lubricant/reuse rules, storage and post-change qualification; mechanical |
| Receive/camera/probe modules and tether assemblies | Replaceable serial-linked calibrated chain where selected | Compatible revisions, recalibration and privacy/data handling, strain relief; acquisition/mechanical/electrical |

| Hub/controller/radio/storage | Reference CPU/MCU/radio are not a frozen service BOM | Supported target/image, secure reprovision/revoke, regional antenna configuration and restoration; security/firmware/RF |
| Mount/fastener/isolation/packaging kits | Revision-matched non-pressure components | Loads/material/corrosion, torque, approved transport protection; mechanical/quality |

For each released spare record exact SKU/revision and permitted substitutions, supplier/lead time, actual consumption and failure data, reorder trigger/basis, minimum stock/quantity (currently null), shelf-life/FIFO or expiry control, storage, test/calibration requirement, cost/quote reference and support availability. Quarantine obsolete or expired stock; do not consume it to meet an unfunded service promise. Fund quantities from measured demand and lead time, not fabricated field failure rates. Active projector/wiper spares remain excluded from the passive configuration.

## 4. Warranty and service terms (`WAR-01`)

No price, warranty duration, response SLA or commercial offer is approved. Before launch, product/legal/service owners must define seller/manufacturer roles, jurisdictions/customer classes, mandatory rights, any separate commercial warranty, covered variants/serials, documented permitted use, intake/contact route, response/repair/replacement/refund rules as applicable, shipping/retrieval costs and exclusions reviewed for legality. A voluntary term must not purport to remove mandatory customer rights.

Use a warranty case to link purchase/delivery evidence, declared terms/version at sale, symptom, safety/security triage, coverage decision with reason and legal review where needed, work/parts costs, customer communications, disposition and closure. Warranty rejection does not close a safety incident, stop statutory duties or delete evidence. Do not promise support capacity until budget, stock and competent service arrangements exist.

## 5. Product incidents, corrective action and recall (`INC-01`, `REC-01`)

Service coordinator triages all injury, fire/battery, ingress, lost gear, entanglement, ecological disturbance, unintended output, permit breach, serious reliability and misleading-data reports with the qualified safety/compliance/security owners. Do not assign a legal reporting threshold or deadline without the dated applicability decision; applicable reporting duties may run before full root-cause confirmation.

1. Register discovery and occurrence times separately, reporter/affected serials and location in restricted records, allegation versus evidence, immediate hazards, known operating/configuration state and evidence preservation. Escalate urgent danger through the approved local emergency process; no emergency contact or authorized response team is invented here.
2. Safety owner sets containment: hold WIP/stock/release, evaluate safe inhibit/use restrictions and retrieval, segregate suspect parts and assess adjacent units. Compliance owner determines reportability, competent bodies, legal clock trigger/deadline, required contents and authorized submitter. Log actual reports/acknowledgements only when sent under separate authority; draft is not sent.
3. Trace affected population through lot/serial, BOM, firmware/configuration, calibration, fixture and service histories. Unknown traceability widens containment pending evidence; do not exclude unlocated or offline units from exposure counts. Preserve records and chain of custody.
4. Investigate failure mechanism and contributing causes with specialists; use controlled tests only after authorization. Record confirmed cause, uncertainty, corrective/preventive action, change/requalification plan and residual risk acceptance. A software fix does not automatically resolve physical or ecological harm.
5. The authorized product/compliance/safety owners decide field correction, stop sale/use, repair, replacement, withdrawal or recall and required authority/customer notices. The recall record identifies affected serial range/count and reconciliation basis, contact plan, approved message/version, remedy, logistics/budget, battery/privacy constraints, regulatory decisions and evidence retention.
6. Track each unit as uncontacted/contacted/located/corrected/returned/scrapped/unresolved with actual timestamps and proof. Measure reach and remedy completion against the identified population; unknown whereabouts remain unresolved. Reconcile stock and repaired configurations, verify corrective action and require independent closure. Do not close a recall from notices sent alone.

Before launch, conduct authorized tabletop trace/incident/recall drills using conspicuously synthetic identities, then validate actual traceability in PVT. Retain drill inputs, gaps, time measurements, corrective actions and reviewer acceptance separately from incidents. No drill or real recall was executed here.

## 6. Vulnerability and cyber-incident response (`VULN-01`)

**Owner:** designated security responder with a funded backup; compliance adviser decides applicable law, reportability and current deadlines; firmware/platform owners build signed fixes; product owner authorizes external communications/releases. No security contact address, staffed service, reporting submission or legal approval is created by this procedure.

Before launch, approve and publish a monitored intake route, safe handling/disclosure policy, supported-version list, response targets, escalation/backup coverage and legal reporting playbook. The roadmap identifies an imminent CRA transition; use the assurance compliance lane's dated official-source applicability decision at the actual event date, not a hard-coded assumption that all obligations are future or identical for every variant.

For each report: record receipt/discovery/awareness times and sources, affected components/SBOM versions and serials, exploitation indicators, confidentiality/access restrictions, severity rationale, safety/privacy interaction, and mitigation status. Preserve reproducible evidence without collecting credentials or running an exploit on live devices. Compliance records legal clock start, applicable duty, deadline, competent reporting route, authorized submitter, submission receipt and follow-up status; unknown applicability escalates promptly rather than waiting for technical certainty.

Contain compromised credentials/stations/releases using approved revocation/rotation procedures and identify the affected fleet. Evaluate fail-silent/diagnostic behavior under lost credentials/connectivity; disabling a cloud account is not proof that remote hazardous hardware is isolated. Test a fix on reference/simulation first, then the authorized hardware regression/update/interruption route. Record signed artifact and SBOM/configuration hashes, security floor, compatibility, recovery, staged rollout/stop criteria and independent review. Preserve evidence; do not silently backdate or hide a failed update.

Coordinate advisories/disclosure and legally required reports through authorized owners. Track attempted versus acknowledged installation and actual installed versions, including offline devices; a pushed update is not an applied fix. Closure requires affected-population disposition, verified mitigation, notification/reporting completion where applicable and lessons linked to change control. A report remains open where unsafe unpatched units or unknown fleet coverage persist.

## 7. End of support, transfer and disposal (`EOS-01`)

Before commercial authorization define support start/end basis, jurisdiction-specific obligations, declared service life, supported targets, security update period, funded staff/spares/hosting/calibration capacity, renewal/dependency risks and customer communications. All durations and dates are currently null. Legal obligations, expected use and actual support capacity govern the decision; never use a convenient arbitrary date or permit a notice to waive applicable duties.

For retirement/transfer record affected serials and versions, approved notice schedule and language, unsupported functions, migration/export method, final supported signed image and recovery, remaining incident/vulnerability handling, key/certificate revocation or transfer through authorized secure process, data retention/export/deletion proof, and service/spares availability. Test loss of hosting/time/certificate services and any decommissioning behavior under an approved method; remote disappearance must not cause output or unsafe recovery.

Qualified site/marine owners approve physical removal and lost-equipment response. Environmental/compliance owners determine battery/electronics waste classification, authorized waste route and custody receipts. Verify sanitized storage and revoked identities before resale/scrap, retaining legally required traceability separately from customer media. Do not publish an end date, delete operational data, disable a device, contact a customer or move equipment under this procedure without separate authorization.
