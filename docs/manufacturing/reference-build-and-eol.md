# Reference manufacturing procedure: incoming, assembly and end of line

Document `MFG-01`, revision 0.2, 2026-09-08. Status: **procedure only; not authorized for physical execution or release**. No incoming inspection, assembly, energization, calibration, end-of-line test or shipment has been performed for this procedure.

## 1. Controlled reference and hold rules

Use [reference-v1 / HW-REF-1.1](../../hardware/interfaces/reference-v1.json) and [hardware decisions](../hardware/reference-decisions.md) as provisional inputs, not released manufacturing instructions. Bind their actual hashes in each future work order; a matching revision string alone is insufficient. A changed interface requires impact review before reuse of this procedure.

The baseline is passive monitoring. The hub, battery/solar and radios are above water; wet receive/camera/probe interfaces are wired. No amplifier, projector or powered wiper is populated. `CPU-01` is a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B 4GB reference; `MCU-01` is ESP32-DevKitC-32E and `RADIO-01` is a RAK3272S/RAK3172 EU868 candidate. These are not approved purchases. Exact wet housing, receive chain, camera/tether, battery/charger/protection, connectors, gateway, production firmware, calibration and allowed environment are unresolved. Nominal rail values are not acceptance limits. Unverified CAD allocations and logical GPIO reservations are not vendor dimensions or released connector pinouts; the source-verified positions/envelopes below still do not release a harness or physical build.

Revision review, 2026-09-08: `HW-REF-1.1` supersedes `HW-REF-1.0`; this procedure is explicitly rebound after reading the interface's `revision_changes`. ESP32 GPIO17/J3.11 TX and GPIO16/J3.12 RX map to RAK J4.8 RX and J4.7 TX; RAK power/return positions are J5.9/J5.7, and revision-dependent J4.9 must not be used as a shared-revision supply. Pin functions are source-verified, not released wiring or power. The battery reference envelope is now XYZ 188 × 147 × 199 mm with an additional provisional 40 mm terminal service allowance; manual/drawing width reconciliation remains open. The panel envelope is 668 × 425 × 25 mm. The inspected 40 W panel row has no discontinued footnote; the earlier adjacent-row interpretation was withdrawn, but current availability remains unverified. These are hardware-source updates, not this procedure's independent vendor certification. Fit, cable/service space and power/process effects still require qualified review and physical proof. No new physical approval follows; all tests and forms remain unperformed.

Authority comes from separate approved work orders, competent people, budget and applicable facility/site permissions, not this document. For this tranche, execute only document review and reference software/simulation. Do not connect physical equipment. Future physical work stops when a required part, limit, tool calibration, approval or instruction is missing. Record `blocked`, quarantine the affected material/unit and open a nonconformance; do not choose a convenient value. No pressure-test instructions or acoustic-output settings are supplied here.

Read the [requirements](../requirements/production-requirements.md), [hazards](../safety/initial-hazard-register.md), [physical-test handoff](../qualification/procedures/physical-test-record.md), [traceability procedure](traceability-and-records.md) and [EVT/DVT/PVT gates](../qualification/procedures/evt-dvt-pvt.md). ## 2. Incoming inspection (`IN-01`, hardware `PV-01`)

**Owners:** receiving assembler records facts; quality reviewer disposes the lot; mechanical/electrical/acoustics owners approve characteristics in their domains. No self-approved substitution.

1. Before accepting delivery into usable stock, obtain authorized order, supplier identity, exact manufacturer part and revision, packing list, supplier lot/date code/serials, quantities, source documents, shelf-life and transport conditions where relevant. With no purchase authorization, this step remains unperformed.
2. Assign a unique receipt ID. Photograph the label and packaging without credentials or personal data. Count received, damaged, segregated, sampled, accepted and rejected items separately; reconcile the physical count to the receipt. Mark unidentified or suspect parts `quarantined`, never `accepted` by absence of a defect report.
3. Check part/revision against the approved BOM and source provenance. Supplier certificate identifiers and hashes are recorded as source evidence, not automatically accepted conformity or calibration of the full system. Preserve accessible source copies and dates. A catalogue substitute or changed vendor drawing opens change review.
4. Inspect using the characteristic sheet below. The engineering/quality owners must first freeze sampling basis, lot definition, acceptance/rejection rule, dimensions/tolerances, instruments and calibration validity. Unknown critical characteristics block the affected item. Do not assume an AQL, inspect only convenient pieces or treat an unexamined lot as passed. Per-unit identity and critical traceability cannot be replaced by statistical sampling.
5. Record each sampled item's identity, raw observation or measurement, units, uncertainty where relevant, instrument ID, calibration validity, method revision, criterion source, operator and date. Record `not_measured` rather than zero for absent measurements. Link photos and reports by hash. A failed sample holds the lot until approved disposition defines containment and any expanded inspection.
6. Quality signs an accepted/rejected/quarantined disposition with quantity and storage restrictions. Separate rejected stock physically and in inventory. Store seals, batteries, optics and electronics under the approved supplier conditions; missing conditions block usable-stock release.

| Characteristic sheet | Reference input and required checks | Missing decision that blocks acceptance | Hazards |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPU/controller/radio boards | `CPU-01`, `MCU-01`, `RADIO-01`; exact model/revision, connectors, damage, authenticity source, mounting datums, regional radio/antenna combination | Approved BOM, vendor drawing tolerances, board/region revision and anti-counterfeit/source policy | HAZ-005, 014–017 |
| Wet boundary, seals, window, penetrators | Exact matched vendor assembly, material/finish, seal lot and shelf-life, surface damage, dimensions and certificate scope | Exact SKU and vendor assembly instructions; marine depth/time compatibility; scratch/defect limits | HAZ-005, 007 |
| Cable/harness/connector | `H-01`–`H-06`; actual wire/insulation markings, connector keying and pin-view drawing, lengths, strain-relief parts, shield termination | Released gauge, ampacity, bend radius, pinout, safe inspection method and limits | HAZ-005, 008, 016 |
| Battery, charging, protection and solar | Matched pack/BMS/charger/converter/fuse part identities; shipment condition, date codes and documentation | Exact chemistry/SKU, transport classification, handling/storage limits and coordinated design; nominal 12 V is insufficient | HAZ-005, 006 |
| Mounts, fasteners, isolators and printable non-pressure parts | Material/finish/lot, print or machining process revision, datum dimensions and visible defects | Released drawings/tolerances, corrosion isolation, load role and rejection criteria; no printed pressure barrier or primary mooring assumption | HAZ-007–009 |
| Hydrophone, DAQ, camera and probes | Exact chain components and serials, source data and genuine calibration certificate scope/validity if supplied | Receive band, gain, clock, cable/power and per-chain calibration requirements | HAZ-002, 010, 011, 013 |

## 3. Assembly traveler (`AT-01`, hardware `PV-02`, `PV-06`, `PV-14`)

Create one traveler per actual unit and retain links to child subassemblies. Serial format is a future quality decision; templates intentionally have no issued serial. Work order identifies EVT, DVT or PVT, authorized quantity, exact configuration and document/fixture set. Never assemble directly from illustrative CAD or this generic sequence without released detailed instructions.

Each step records start/finish time, operator, consumed part/lot/quantity, drawing/work-instruction hash, tool/fixture ID and calibration where needed, observations/raw evidence, deviation ID, result, and witness for defined hold points. Blank steps are not passes. Define independent inspection roles and hold points before work starts.

| Step | Work and concrete record | Hold point / acceptance source |
|---|---|---|
| AT-01.1 Kit and identify | Reconcile accepted incoming lots to unit BOM; allocate unit/subassembly serials; label test status and segregate research variants. Record actual component population. | All parts released; no substitution, missing lot or expired shelf-life. Passive unit has no projector/amplifier/wiper drive. |
| AT-01.2 Prepare safe workstation | Record authorized method, ESD/tool controls, handling hazards and isolation state. Review battery and stored-energy hazards with qualified owner. | No energized work by default; safe isolation/discharge verification follows approved electrical method, never an assumed wait time. |
| AT-01.3 Inert mechanical fit | Fit purchased non-energized components using approved drawings. Record mating features, fastener stack, cable exits, tool access and service travel. | Hardware `PV-01`/`PV-02` and actual tolerance/torque sources. Do not force fit, drill the wet boundary or trim a part as undocumented rework. |
| AT-01.4 Harness installation | Reconcile wire IDs to `H-01`–`H-06`; record connector/pin-view revision, polarity/keying, shield/return/isolation and strain relief. | Qualified electrical approval of released wiring; provisional GPIO allocations or null connectors block wiring. `H-07` remains absent in passive configuration. |
| AT-01.5 Controlled fastening/sealing | Record approved torque value/unit/source, measured setting/tool, fastener/locking material lots; record seal identity, inspection, approved lubricant/process and witness. | Exact supplier/engineer instruction and limits required. No invented torque, grease, potting recipe, cure schedule or seal-reuse rule. Unknown values stop closure. |
| AT-01.6 Pre-energization inspection | Record independent drawing-to-build review and approved continuity/polarity/isolation observations. | Hardware `PV-06`; safe test voltage and method must be approved. No generic insulation test on connected electronics. No power applied in this tranche. |
| AT-01.7 Software and identity handoff | Bind hardware target, software/firmware image hashes, config hash and credential references using `MFG-02`. Record installed versus merely intended images separately. | Released provisioning fixture, authorized credentials, signed target-compatible image and recovery path required for physical provisioning. No flashing in this tranche. |
| AT-01.8 Calibration and EOL | Link chain calibration and execute the approved per-unit EOL route below. Preserve first attempt and every failure/retest. | Qualification sample tests cannot replace per-unit checks. Calibration status remains unknown until actual proof exists. |
| AT-01.9 Close-out | Reconcile components, route completion, NCRs, calibration, EOL and labels; record as-built mass/configuration where required. Independent quality review signs internal disposition. | Passing internal EOL alone cannot authorize shipment, deployment, CE marking or an active claim. Commercial release remains blocked by separate gates. |

No physical build is claimed by publishing this route. A second assembler must later reproduce the detailed package without unlogged designer guidance; see PVT procedure.

## 4. End-of-line route (`EOL-01`)

Quality and specialist owners must approve the exact variant-specific EOL matrix, sequence, fixture validation, duration, uncertainty/guard-band decision rule and limits before a work order. Reconcile the specimen, as-built configuration, calibrated tools, current firmware/configuration and affected NCRs at entry. Unknown or changed test conditions block the test; automatic or simulated `pass` results are not physical evidence.

| Check | Required per-unit record and method | Owner / dependency | Current physical status |
|---|---|---|---|
| EOL-ID | Scan/read serials, BOM lots, revision/hash set, labels and passive population; compare physical build to traveler | Quality; `IN-01`, `AT-01` | Not performed |
| EOL-ELEC | Approved visual/polarity/continuity/isolation checks and safe power-state measurements against released circuit and instrument limits | Electrical; `PV-06`/`PV-07` | Not performed; limits unresolved |
| EOL-INHIBIT | In passive variant, inspect absence of output stage/projector/powered wiper and record interface restrictions. Any future active variant requires its own approved independent stop/startup/fault EOL route | Electrical/safety; `PV-11` for conditional active route | Not performed; software inhibit is not hardware proof |
| EOL-BOOT | Identify installed target/images; measure boot, storage health and approved controlled shutdown/restart behavior; retain logs and failures | Firmware/platform/electrical; approved safe power route | Not performed on hardware |
| EOL-IDENTITY | Verify unique registered device/public identity, approved authentication test, credential policy/version and audit-log secrecy; do not export raw secrets | Security; `MFG-02`, approved provisioning station | Not performed on devices |
| EOL-RECEIVE | Apply specialist-approved reference input; record full-chain serials, calibration validity, gain/response and drop/clock health. Synthetic replay only proves software plumbing | Acoustics/acquisition; `PV-09` | Not performed; no calibrated acoustic claim |
| EOL-VISION | Use approved target and time reference to record focus/visibility, frame loss, offset/drift and uncertainty, including unusable/missing video | Acquisition; `PV-09`, frozen timing criteria | Not performed |
| EOL-PROBES/RF | Match wired probe and regional radio/gateway configuration; approved sensor/reference checks and permitted RF method record units, uncertainty and received messages | Electrical/acquisition/RF; `PV-13`, no emission by default | Not performed |
| EOL-WET | Apply only the competent facility's approved per-unit leak/closure screening if required by the qualification plan; record exact wet assembly and linked raw report | Mechanical/facility; `PV-03` | Not performed; no pressure recipe here |
| EOL-RECOVERY | Confirm approved update/recovery behavior for installed image; DVT destructive/interruption matrix is not blindly repeated at EOL | Security/firmware; accepted DVT route | Not performed on hardware |
| EOL-PACK | Inspect approved packaging, preservation, label identity and included configuration-bound instructions | Quality/compliance; packaging validation below | Not performed |

For each check capture criterion revision before the attempt, actual input, expected behavior, measured value/unit/uncertainty, raw report hash, start/end times, instrument IDs, result and reviewer. Time/duty/temperature/voltage/audio limits must come from specialist documents, not this table. A test station must detect a known failing reference/fault under its authorized method before reliance; a station self-test is not a unit test result. Out-of-calibration equipment triggers containment and retrospective impact review of all affected serials.

Reject a unit if a required step fails or is missing; route to NCR. After approved rework, repeat the affected test and any regression route selected by engineering. Do not erase the first attempt, average away failures, mark a retest as first-pass, bypass interlocks or relax a limit after seeing results. Quality closes the route only with resolved deviations, traceability, actual results and independent approval. All physical EOL proofs are currently missing.

## 5. Nonconformance and rework (`NCR-01`)

1. Stop the affected step. Mark unit/lot/station on hold and preserve failure logs, photos, raw measurements and software/configuration state. Record immediate containment and whether other serials or shipped units could be affected.
2. Open a uniquely identified NCR: requirement and characteristic violated (or missing), discovery stage, as-found state, lot/serials, evidence, severity assessed by the qualified owner, and suspected cause explicitly distinguished from confirmed cause.
3. Quality and the relevant engineer choose return-to-supplier, approved rework, scrap, further investigation or a controlled deviation. The product owner alone cannot waive safety, permission, legal, security or ecological requirements. Unknown safe limits block work; `use-as-is` is not a route around them.
4. Approve an exact rework instruction before touching the unit: replace/adjust operation, tools, replacement part/lot, maximum repetitions, cumulative affected cycles, seal/fastener reuse restrictions, damage limits, retest/requalification scope, author and independent reviewer. **Every presently unknown rework limit blocks that operation.** No default one-time repair, unlimited reflash, repotting, solder touch-up or seal reuse is authorized.
5. Record each attempt and consumed parts, including scrap and labor. Do not reset serial history or calibration age when an enclosure is reopened. Pressure-boundary, cable, power, sensor/gain/clock, firmware or credential changes trigger the change controls in `MFG-02` and `MFG-03`.
6. Perform approved post-rework verification; if required repetition or damage limits are reached, stop and return to engineering. Independent quality review closes the NCR only after containment, cause/action and verification are evidenced. Preserve unresolved disposition as blocked. Link recurring defects to corrective action and service/recall assessment.

## 6. Packaging and storage (`PKG-01`)

Packaging is unqualified. Mechanical, battery/transport and compliance specialists must set exact packaging materials, ESD/moisture/corrosion protection, connector caps, seal/storage conditions, orientation, shock/vibration/compression/drop/environment test methods and limits for the configured unit. Do not infer a lithium transport classification, UN test summary, air-freight eligibility or shipping label from the provisional battery chemistry.

Before later packing: verify quality hold status; approved safe energy state; clean/dry method and residue restrictions; caps/strain protection; isolated heavy items; tamper/serial label matching; approved software/calibration versions; contents/instructions/languages and no unsupported pressure/CE/efficacy marking. Record packaging BOM/lot, unit and battery configuration, storage expiry/inspection dates, package identification, mass and dimensions as measured, photos and packer/reviewer. Never energize or ship to validate packing.

Before release: link representative transport-test reports to the exact packaged variant, inspect and rerun the approved post-transport functional/leak route, and investigate damage. Any carton, cushioning, battery, geometry, mass distribution or shipping mode change needs impact review. An unopened box or clean photograph is not packaging qualification. Actual shipment requires separate commercial, transport and destination permissions.
