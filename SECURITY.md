# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email basic@intrface.eu with the affected revision, a concise description, and reproduction steps. Do not post credentials, personal data or exploit details in a public issue. We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days and coordinate disclosure after triage and a reviewed fix. This is not a bug bounty program; no payment is offered. Please do not test against third-party systems or live farms without authorization.

## Scope and supported versions

Reports about this repository's software, development tools, hardware design files and documentation are in scope. There is no released or deployed product and no supported-release or security-update window yet. Development checks do not establish field readiness, regulatory conformity or operational security support. For the proposed supply process, see the [vulnerability intake process](docs/compliance/software-supply-chain/vulnerability-intake.md) and [support-window statement](docs/compliance/software-supply-chain/support-window.md).

The [local software inventory and fixed-pattern secrets scan](docs/compliance/software-supply-chain/inventory-process.md) are limited development checks, not a security audit or release authorization.
