 K1/K2 are independent normally-open power disconnects controlled ONLY by an
 independent selected safety controller, not Pi/ESP GPIO or a software latch.
 Review interruption of BOTH conductors for a floating supply. No spare feed,
 audio input, shield or USB connection may back-power the disconnected chain.
 Passive HUB diagnostics have a separate fused path and can remain powered.

 Concept control inputs to independent safety controller [NO terminal allocation]
   physical key permission
   NC physical stop channel A (open wire = stop)
   NC physical stop channel B (open wire = stop)
   independent hardware window-watchdog HEALTHY contact
   K1/K2 monitored auxiliary feedback / external-device monitoring
   LOCAL monitored normally-open manual REARM button

 Rearm requires healthy channels/feedback/watchdog AND a released button followed
 by a fresh local press. Holding a button through power return, stop release or
 watchdog recovery must NOT rearm. Software/GPIO may veto; cannot close K1/K2
 by itself. Static heartbeat is not healthy; internal MCU watchdog is not the
 independent watchdog. Coil suppression must not defeat the measured stop bound.

 Fails-off on control-power loss, either NC stop opening, watchdog loss, invalid
 contact feedback or key removal. Contact weld/cross-fault coverage and the exact
 controller, DC contacts, coil supply and safety level require specialist design.

 Stored amplifier/filter/cable energy requires an independent discharge/dump
 path and measured time-to-safe. Opening supply alone is NOT proof of immediate
 acoustic silence. Default-low amplifier enable is supplementary, never the
 sole inhibit. No acoustic frequency/level/TVR/impedance or safe dose selected.
 No failed Lim pinger profile adopted. No electrical or acoustic test performed.

 GROUNDING / WIRING RULES
 Dedicated insulated DC returns; no frame/shield/seawater load return.
 System frame/negative/shore-earth bonds are OPEN, not implicitly shorted.
 Converter isolation rating is a component withstand test, not system isolation.
 Track USB/Ethernet/RF/analog shields that cross isolation boundaries.
 FG, exposed metal, lightning/surge and saltwater corrosion need site review.
 Cable ratings, contact ratings, wire gauge, fuse curve/breaking capacity,
 connector locking, crimp/torque and pull-test requirements remain unresolved.
 Never substitute a passing ampacity inequality for fault clearing qualification.
